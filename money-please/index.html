Scanning

How do I get a good scan?
Lay the receipt flat in good light and fit the whole thing in the frame, top to total. Creases and curls are usually fine; glare and shadows across the numbers are what trip it up.
It says the items don't add up to the total.
The app checks its reading against the subtotal and total printed on the receipt. When they disagree, one line was misread or missed. Tap the line that's wrong to fix it, add the missing item, or retake the photo. Swipe left on the receipt to compare with the original photo.
Can I scan a photo I already took?
Yes. Tap Choose from library on the home screen.
Does it work with other currencies?
Yes, including currencies without cents, like yen. It's tuned on English-language receipts, so others may need more fixing by hand.

Splitting

How are shared items split?
Tap everyone who shared a dish and it's divided between them. If the split leaves an odd cent, it goes to whoever has taken the fewest odd cents so far, so one person doesn't absorb every rounding error.
How are tax and tip divided?
Your choice on the Summary screen: evenly across everyone, or by what each person ordered.
I forgot the tip, or a name. Can I fix a split after tapping Done?
Yes. Your most recent split has an Edit button on the home screen. With Pro, you can edit any past split from History.

Pro and payments

What does Pro add?
Your Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal on the shared card, group texts to everyone with their share, adding people from Contacts, and split history. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
I bought Pro on another phone, or reinstalled.
Tap History (the clock on the home screen) to open the Pro screen, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.
Does the app send or collect money?
No. It works out who owes what and gives everyone your payment links. The money moves in Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal, never through the app.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. Receipts are read on the device, nothing is uploaded, and there's no account. See the privacy policy.
