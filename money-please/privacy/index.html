Money, Please!

Privacy Policy

Money, Please! does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

The app contains no networking code of any kind. Everything it knows stays on your device.

What the app stores

Receipt photos and scans
Photos you take or choose are read on your device by Apple's Vision framework to recognize text. The photo is used for the split you're working on. It is not uploaded anywhere and is not saved to any server.
Split history
Completed splits — the merchant, the items, who owed what, and the tax and tip breakdown — are saved locally so you can look them up later. You can delete any split, delete all of them, or set them to auto-delete after 30 or 90 days, from the History screen.
Your payment handles
If you enter a Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal handle, it is stored locally as a preference so you don't have to retype it. It is only ever shown to people you choose to share a split with.
Contacts
If you grant Contacts access, names and phone numbers are read directly on your device to help you add people to a split. They are never uploaded, and the app only reads them while you are picking someone. Contacts access is optional — you can always add people by typing their names.

Deleting the app removes all of this.

What leaves your device

Only the things you explicitly send:

Sharing a split
When you tap share or send a group text, iOS's own share sheet or Messages app sends what you chose, to the people you chose. The app keeps no separate copy and has no server.
Payment links
Tapping a Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal link opens that company's app or website. What happens there is governed by their privacy policy, not this one. Money, Please! does not process, move, or hold money — it works out who owes what and hands you the links.
Purchases
The one-time Pro unlock is handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple tells the app whether you own it. We never see your payment details, and we operate no server that could.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
